### Store Management

Lives in `/manage/`

example `/<storeid>/manage/delivery/<day>/` shows html of slots and vans for that day


### The data it needs, writes

1. POST Create Store
2. POST,DELETE Create,Delete Route
3. POST Create Order
4. GET Inventory
5. GET Product Details
6. POST Update Product Stock
7. POST Add van
8. UPDATE Van
9. DELETE Van
